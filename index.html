About the director

Ben-David O. Otitoola — known across the industry as Young Ocha — trained as a cinematographer at Del York Creative Academy, one of Nigeria's film schools built specifically for working on real sets, not just theory. Under his own company, Minds Eyes Tel-D-Vision, he directs and shoots for clients who want their story handled with the same care a feature film gets: deliberate light, considered movement, sound that matters as much as picture.

Off set, Young Ocha produces music — writing hooks, arranging beats and mixing tracks for many of the same artists he shoots videos for. One person carrying both the sound and the picture means the video is built around what the record actually says, instead of a look applied after the fact by someone who never heard the song.
